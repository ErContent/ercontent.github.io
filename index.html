    The story
    
        
    napwnli is the CTF team of the University of Naples Federico II. It was born in 2024 from the Naples finalists of CyberChallenge.IT: students who didn’t want the competition to end with the national final.
    
        
    We play CTFs all over the world: web, pwn, crypto, reversing, forensics, OSINT and Attack/Defense. I’m the investigator of the squad.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    The donkey
    
        
    Our crest is a donkey, like Napoli’s historic mascot, ’o ciuccio. The name is a pun too: Napoli + pwn, the word for taking control of a system.
    
        
    We don’t believe in coincidences: we investigate them.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Federico II
    
        
    We represent the University of Naples Federico II, founded in 1224, one of the oldest public universities in the world. We train in its labs and share what we learn with the next CyberChallenge.IT students.
